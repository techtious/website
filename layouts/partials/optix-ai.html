AI-Native FinOps

Optix AI

Transform cloud cost analysis from manual drudgery into intelligent, actionable insights in seconds.

Optix AI is Techtious's flagship FinOps product: an AI-native agent that connects to your AWS account and delivers ranked, actionable cost optimization recommendations—automatically. No complex dashboards. No manual spreadsheet analysis. Just one command: optix

Unlike traditional FinOps tools that show you charts, Optix uses large language models (Claude or Gemini, your choice) to reason about your costs, spot inefficiencies, and tell you exactly what to optimize and why. Built for engineers, SREs, and FinOps practitioners who want real answers, not noise.

Privacy first. Your AWS credentials stay on your machine. Your cost data is never stored on our servers. You control everything.

Core Capabilities

Cost Breakdown

Identifies top-spending AWS services over the last 30 days so you know exactly where your budget is going. Service-by-service clarity in seconds.

Trend Analysis

Compares month-over-month spending across three months to surface cost drift before it spirals. Spot trends that point to waste.

EC2 Rightsizing

Detects oversized instances and calculates exact monthly savings per recommendation. Easy wins your team can implement immediately.

Savings Plans Intelligence

Recommends committed-use discount opportunities aligned with your actual usage patterns. No guessing—AI-backed precision.

Anomaly Detection

Flags unexpected cost spikes in real-time using intelligent thresholds. Catch budget overruns before finance gets the surprise.

Multi-Account Support

Manage and analyze multiple AWS accounts from a single CLI. Perfect for consultancies and organizations with complex AWS structures.

Tag Governance

Discover and audit resource tags for compliance and cost allocation. Ensure your tagging strategy supports accurate chargeback.

HTML Report Export

Generate polished HTML reports for client sharing. Professional output ready for stakeholder presentations and finance teams.

Multi-Model Support

Works with Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, or DeepSeek. Bring your own API key, zero data residency concerns, zero vendor lock-in.

Every Report Includes

  • Executive Summary — total spend, key findings, and top priorities. Perfect for sharing with your finance team.
  • Service Cost Landscape — complete AWS service breakdown with 3-month trends. See exactly where money flows.
  • AI-Ranked Recommendations — prioritized by estimated impact. Implementation difficulty flagged. Savings calculated.
  • Anomaly Alerts — unexpected spikes flagged with context. When it happened. What triggered it. What to investigate.
  • Action Plan — step-by-step implementation guide. Your team can start optimizing in the next sprint.
